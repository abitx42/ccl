# 08 — Privacy, Consent and Ethics

This site will show real villagers, named officials and sometimes vulnerable places. Getting this right matters more than any feature.

## Core Rules

1. **Consent Gating**: Written or recorded informed consent is required before publishing anyone's name, role, or photo.
2. **Minors**: Identifiable children require guardian consent. Default is to omit identifiable minors.
3. **Sensitive Ecological & Cultural Locations**: Rare species nesting grounds and sacred sites are given coarse coordinates only.
4. **No Raw Survey Data**: Household surveys are aggregated and anonymized before publishing.
5. **No AI Hallucinations**: No invented quotes, statistics, or synthetic field photos.
6. **Publishing Checklist**:
   - [ ] Facts and figures verified by the district lead
   - [ ] All people in text/photos have consent recorded
   - [ ] Sensitive locations coarsened
   - [ ] Community/village reviewed and approved
   - [ ] Translations checked by a fluent reader
   - [ ] Alt text and credits complete

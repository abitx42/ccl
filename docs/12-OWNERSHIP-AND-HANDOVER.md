# 12 — Ownership and Handover

The site is meant to outlive the project team. This doc defines who owns what, records where accounts live, and defines handover when funding ends.

## Ownership Chain

- **University owner (institution)**: Accountable for site and data custody.
- **Project steward (named person)**: Day-to-day owner; holds evidence register.
- **Technical maintainer**: Builds, dependencies, CI/CD.
- **Content editors / district leads**: Maintain verified records.
- **Backup owner**: Named successor.

## Preservation & End-of-Funding Runbook

1. Freeze content; publish final release and Zenodo snapshot with DOI.
2. Keep static site online (Cloudflare Pages free tier) with archive banner.
3. Replace forms and store links with permanent contact address.
4. Transfer private data to university research repository.
